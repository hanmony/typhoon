Abstract: The typhoon weather has a great influence on the organization of subway operation. It is of positive significance to discuss the timing of the outage and recovery of subway lines under typhoon weather. The article summarizes the regulations of the Guangzhou Metro and other industry regulations, and proposes three different outage and recovery timing plans. It also conducts comparative analysis and summarizes a set of outage and recovery opportunities suitable for the Guangzhou Metro in combination with operational characteristics. To reduce the adverse impact of typhoon weather on the subway operation organization and maximize passenger travel requirements on the premise of ensuring safety.
